Kitchen Remodeling in Hamilton, NJ

The kitchen is where your family gathers, where holidays happen, and where you spend more time than any other room in the house. It deserves more than a cookie-cutter renovation. At Hamilton Kitchens, we design and build kitchens that fit your home, your style, and the way you actually live.

Services

Our Kitchen Remodeling Services

Complete Kitchen Renovations

Full gut remodels including layout changes, new cabinetry, countertops, lighting, flooring, and full plumbing and electrical coordination.

Custom & Semi-Custom Cabinetry

As an authorized Wood-Mode dealer, we offer fully custom cabinetry built to your exact specifications, along with semi-custom and value lines for a range of budgets.

Countertops & Backsplashes

Quartz, granite, marble, quartzite, solid surface, and butcher block, paired with tile or stone backsplashes that tie the whole room together. We'll help you choose the right materials for your cooking habits and maintenance preferences.

Islands, Pantries & Storage Solutions

Pull-outs, drawer organizers, appliance garages, walk-in pantries, and custom islands that add workspace and seating.

Process

How Our Process Works

  1. Showroom Visit & Consultation

    Come see our eight full kitchen displays and talk with a designer about your goals, your budget, and your timeline.

  2. In-Home Measurement

    We visit your home to take precise measurements and evaluate the space, plumbing, electrical, and structure.

  3. Design & Selections

    Your designer creates a detailed plan using CAD and 3D modeling, so you can see your new kitchen before a single cabinet is ordered. You'll choose door styles, finishes, hardware, and countertops from samples you can hold in your hand.

  4. Detailed Proposal

    You receive a clear, itemized proposal. No vague allowances, no surprise change orders.

  5. Installation

    Our experienced installation team handles the work from demolition to final detail, keeping your home clean and the project on schedule.

  6. Final Walkthrough

    We go through every detail with you before we call it finished, and we stand behind our work after we leave.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Once your design is finalized and installation begins, most kitchens are finished in four to six weeks. Custom cabinetry has its own lead time after design approval, so we'll build that into your overall schedule up front and give you realistic dates before any work starts.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in the Hamilton area?

Every project is different, but costs vary with the size of your kitchen, the materials you choose, and the scope of the work. We'll give you an itemized proposal after your consultation so you know exactly where your investment is going.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical?

Yes. We coordinate licensed plumbers and electricians as part of your project, so every phase is handled and scheduled by our team — you won’t be left chasing separate trades.

Can I stay in my home during the remodel?

Yes. Most homeowners do. We'll help you set up a temporary kitchen and keep the work area contained.
