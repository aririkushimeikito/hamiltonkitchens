About Hamilton Kitchens

Five Decades of Kitchens and Baths, One Family at a Time

Hamilton Kitchens opened its doors in 1973 with a simple idea: treat every home like it's your own, and do the job right the first time. More than fifty years later, that's still how we work.

Over the years we've seen styles come and go, but the things that matter to homeowners haven't changed. You want a company that listens, a design that fits your life, quality materials, skilled installers, and a project that finishes when we said it would.

Hamilton Kitchens is a full-service kitchen and bath design company. From custom and semi-custom cabinetry to countertops and backsplashes, we handle every element of your project under one roof. Our showroom is where it all begins: a place to see the brands and products we carry, talk through ideas, and get a feel for what's possible. From there, the process continues in your home or office, where we take precise measurements and use CAD and 3D modeling to design a space that fits the way you live and work.

How we work

Our Approach

We design around how you live.

Every project starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. We want to know how you cook, entertain, and move through your space before we draw a single line.

We build with materials we'd put in our own homes.

As an authorized Wood-Mode dealer, we offer some of the finest custom cabinetry made in America, along with a full range of semi-custom and stock cabinetry lines and countertop materials to fit every budget.

We respect your home.

Our installers show up on time, keep the job site clean, and treat your family with courtesy. A remodel is disruptive enough without a messy crew.

We stay on schedule.

We give you a realistic timeline before work begins, and we keep our word. Once your design is finalized and installation begins, most projects are complete in four to six weeks. Our team handles every phase of the remodel, so there's no waiting on outside trades.

Licensed, Insured, and Local

Hamilton Kitchens is a fully licensed and insured New Jersey home improvement contractor (License #13VH02227600). We've maintained an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and are proud to have built much of our business on referrals from satisfied customers.
