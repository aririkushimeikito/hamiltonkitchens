FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Once installation begins, most bathrooms are complete within four to six weeks, and many finish sooner. Powder rooms are often faster; master bath suites with layout changes can run toward the longer end.

What does a bathroom remodel cost?

Bathroom costs vary with the size of the room, layout changes, and the finishes you choose. Once we see your space and talk through your goals, we’ll give you a clear, itemized proposal — no surprises.

Can you make my bathroom safer for an aging parent without it looking clinical?

Absolutely. Accessible design has come a long way. We regularly integrate curbless showers, grab bars, and comfort-height fixtures into bathrooms that look like they belong in a magazine.
